## How to Redeem Your Code
Since you've been testing the app for a few weeks, please follow these steps to ensure your "Tester" license converts to a "Full" license without losing your data.

### Step 1: Claim Your Code
**Do not uninstall the app yet.** 1. Open the **Google Play Store** app.
2. Tap your **Profile Icon** (top right).
3. Go to **Payments & subscriptions** > **Redeem code**.
4. Enter your unique code and tap **Redeem**.
5. You should see a confirmation that you now "own" the app.

### Step 2: Syncing the License
1. Search for the app in the Play Store. 
2. Instead of a "Price," it should now say **Installed** or **Enable**.
3. **If it still shows a Price:** Tap the price button. The payment window will pop up showing a balance of **$0.00**. Tap **Buy** to finalize the transaction.

---

### ⚠️ Troubleshooting
* **If it asks you to pay full price:** Ensure you are logged into the same Google account you used for testing.
* **If it's stuck:** Go to your phone's **Settings > Apps > Google Play Store > Storage** and tap **Clear Cache**, then restart the Play Store.
